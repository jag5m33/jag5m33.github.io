# About me

I'm Jag, a data analyst based in London and happy to relocate anywhere in the UK. I finished an MSc in Applied Bioinformatics at King's College London with a Distinction, and I'm looking for my first analyst role.

## How I think about data

I've always loved puzzles, jigsaws most of all. You tip out a box of pieces that make no sense, find the edges, sort the colours, and slowly a picture appears. Analysis feels the same to me: messy data first, then structure, then the story.

The habit I'm proudest of is checking where the numbers came from before trusting them. In my MSc project, "unusual" athlete results turned out to be instrument and batch effects. In 7.7 million US road accident records, a drop in severe accidents came from a change in who supplied the data, not from roads getting safer.

## Why there's so much machine learning here

Most analyst roles don't use much machine learning, and I don't expect my first job to either. I study it because I'm curious about it and it's where I'd like to grow over time.

This site is really a record of how I learn. Everything on it I taught myself alongside my degree, written up from my own handwritten notes and tied to a project where I used it. Around 200 people have used these notes. If a role needs a tool I haven't used yet, I'll pick it up the same way.
